“We got you!” George and Charlie exclaimed and high-fived.

“Got me?” said Mike.

“The guys thought we should help you lighten up a bit,” Sulley
 explained. “When was the last time you went out to lunch? Or
 stopped to chat?”

Mike started to explain. Then he realized his friends were right.
 He had been trying so hard to collect laughs that he’d forgotten to
 have some of his own!
