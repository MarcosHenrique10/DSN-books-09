Together, Mulan, Shang, and the others knocked out the
 Hun soldiers and rescued the Emperor. Then Shang went after
 Shan-Yu. Furious that the Emperor had escaped, the Hun leader
 drew his sword.

“You took away my victory,” Shan-Yu growled at Shang.

“No,” said Mulan. “I did.” She pulled back her hair so
 Shan-Yu would recognize her and Shang’s life would be spared.

“The soldier from the mountains!” the Hun leader cried. He
 began to chase her.
