Captain Shang was a skillful leader, and though Mulan felt
 clumsy and inadequate, she worked as hard as she could. She
 didn’t want anyone to find out she was a girl.

Shang led the recruits through a series of grueling training
 exercises to help them prepare for battle. One of the lessons
 involved retrieving an arrow from the top of a pole with a heavy
 bronze disk tied to each wrist.

“One represents discipline, the other strength,” Shang said of
 the disks. “You need both to reach the arrow.”

No one was able to do it. Finally, after several attempts,
 Mulan had an idea. She pulled the weights onto her wrists,
 looped them together, and used them to hoist herself to the top.
 She had done it!

All the soldiers admired Mulan’s determination. And even her
 new friends, Chien-Po, Ling, and Yao, still didn’t know that she
 wasn’t a man.
