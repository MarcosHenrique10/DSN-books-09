Long ago, and far beyond the Great Wall of China, a young
 woman named Fa Mulan lived with her family.

One day, the Emperor’s aide rode into town. “The Huns have
 invaded China!” he announced. “One man from every family must
 serve in the Imperial Army.”

Mulan’s father felt it was his duty to join the army even
 though he had been injured in earlier battles. That night, he
 began to practice some fighting moves, unaware that Mulan was
 watching. Suddenly, a pain from an old wound shot through his
 leg, and he collapsed on the floor.

Mulan knew that her father wouldn’t be able to survive
 another battle, so she decided to disguise herself as a man and
 take his place. She cut her hair, put on soldier’s clothing, and left
 in the middle of the night on her horse, Khan.

But Mulan was not alone. The spirits of her ancestors sent
 Mushu, a tiny dragon, along to protect her.
