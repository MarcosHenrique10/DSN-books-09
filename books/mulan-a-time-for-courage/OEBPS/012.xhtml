Mulan had been wounded,
 but she was still able to jump
 on her horse and pull Shang
 away from the snow just
 before he was buried.

As Chien-Po and the
 others helped them to safety,

Shang looked at Mulan with admiration. “Ping, you are the craziest
 man I’ve ever met. For that, I owe you my life,” he said. “From now
 on you have my trust.”

But Mulan’s wound had to be treated. It was then the soldiers
 learned that she was really a woman.

At that time in China, Mulan’s deception was punishable by
 death. Shang spared her. “A life for a life,” he declared. “My debt is
 repaid.” He told her she would not be allowed to continue on with
 the other soldiers.
