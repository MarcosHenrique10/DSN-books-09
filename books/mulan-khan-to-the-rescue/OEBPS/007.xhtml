“I want to,” Mulan told Khan, “but I’m not allowed! Wait here,
 and I’ll run into the village and get help.”

Khan butted her urgently with his big,
 soft nose.

“I know,” Mulan said, rubbing his
 ears. Oh, it was so frustrating! Mulan
 was trying to be a proper lady, and
 proper ladies did not hop on horses
 and ride to the rescue.

But maybe just
 this once . . .

“You’re right,” Mulan said,
 making a decision. “There
 isn’t enough time to go to
 the village.”

Just this once.
