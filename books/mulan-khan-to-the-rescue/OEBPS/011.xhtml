“I’ll get you down right away!” Mulan promised. But when she
 picked up the ladder, it fell apart in her hands.

“Oh, no,” Mulan said. “The ladder is broken.”

Grandma looked worried. “Maybe you should go get help from
 the village,” she said.

“It would take forever,” Mulan replied. “Don’t worry, Grandma,
 I’ve got this.”
