“You did it!” Grandma cried,
 rushing over to hug Mulan.
 “You brave girl!”

“Well,” Mulan said,
 blushing, “not that brave. I
 mean, I wasn’t the one stuck
 up a tree.”

“You are brave,” Grandma
 said. “Brave and brilliant.”

Mulan and Grandma took
 their time returning home.
 Khan didn’t seem to mind—he
 paused to graze as they went.
