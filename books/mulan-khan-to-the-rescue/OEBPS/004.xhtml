Mulan sighed loudly as she dipped her brush into the pot of
 ink and gently touched it to the paper in front of her. She hated
 practicing calligraphy. It was so boring.

Outside, she heard a horse neigh in the distance. Mulan wished

she was out there, too—
 not stuck inside with the
 musty ink pot and her
 own thoughts.

Ignore it, she told
 herself. Calligraphy,
calligraphy, calligraphy—

NEIGH!

Mulan jumped a
 mile. Her father’s horse,
 Khan, was standing right
 outside her window!
