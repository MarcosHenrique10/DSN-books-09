That evening at dinner, Mulan’s father asked her how her
 studies had gone.

“Actually,” Grandma spoke up, “I helped Mulan with her
 calligraphy today.”

“Oh?” Mulan’s father said. He turned to Mulan. “What
 character did you practice?”

“The character of ‘courage,’” she replied. Mulan and Grandma
 shared a secret smile. “I had a great teacher.”
