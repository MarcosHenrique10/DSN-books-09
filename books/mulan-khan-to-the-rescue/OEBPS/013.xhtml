Mulan looked around. “Okay,” she
 muttered to herself, “let’s see what we
 have to work with. One horse, one
 saddle, two baskets, one bridle . . .
 Aha!” She had the perfect plan.

First she unbuckled Khan’s reins
 from his bridle and tied a piece of her
 sash to them. Then she tied one end of the
 leather strap to a basket.

Mulan tossed the strap up to her grandmother. “Sling it over
 the branch,” she instructed. Then she tied the other end of the
 makeshift rope to Khan’s saddle. Mulan held Khan’s bridle and
 backed him slowly away from the tree. The strap pulled tight, and
 the basket rose into the air until it was right next to Grandma!

“Step into your chariot, Grandma,” Mulan said with a grin.
 Once her grandmother was safe in the basket, Mulan led Khan
 forward step-by-step until the basket was back on the ground.
