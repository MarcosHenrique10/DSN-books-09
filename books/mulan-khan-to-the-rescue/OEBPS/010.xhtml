Soon they arrived at the orchard, and Khan skidded to a stop
 in front of an old cherry tree. Mulan slid down from the saddle.

“Grandma?” she called. She looked all around, but she
 couldn’t see her grandmother. “Grandma!”

“Up here!”

Mulan looked up into the branches of the old cherry tree.

“The ladder fell,” Grandma said. “And I couldn’t climb down.
 I’m so happy to see you, Mulan!”
