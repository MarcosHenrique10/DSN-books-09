“Uh . . .” Ward suddenly felt nervous. He
 darted beneath a floorboard to hide. But he got
 stuck! And when the others tried to pull him out,
 they weren’t strong enough.

“Just go home,” Ward grumbled. “I don’t need
 anyone’s help.”

“No way,” said Nemo. “Remember rule
 number one? Never leave your buddy behind.”

“I’ll bet Bruce could get Ward out,” said Dory.
 “But first we’d have to rescue Bruce.”
