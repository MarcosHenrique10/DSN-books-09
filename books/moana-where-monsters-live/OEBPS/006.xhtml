A giant crab monster named Tamatoa had Maui’s
 hook. Tamatoa lived in Lalotai—the realm of
 monsters—where Maui and Moana were headed.

As Moana struggled upward, Maui said, “Why did
 your people send you?”

“My people didn’t send me,” Moana replied. “The
 ocean did.” But Maui’s question made her wonder,
 could she really complete her quest?
