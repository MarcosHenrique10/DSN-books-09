Mickey calls for help. “Oh, Super Toodles.”

Super Toodles has four Super Mouseketools— a giant blow- dryer,

a catcher’s mitt, a big umbrella, and a Mystery Mouseketool.

“Let’s try the umbrella!” says Mickey.

The big umbrella does the trick. The rubber duckies bounce right off.

But Pete shrinks Minnie’s Bow-tique anyway and zooms off!
