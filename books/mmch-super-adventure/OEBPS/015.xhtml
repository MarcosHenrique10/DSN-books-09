The zeppelin lands and out comes the big boss— Megamort!

“It’s shrinking time,” he says. Megamort makes Pete tiny with his shrink ray.

“Oh, that tickled,” says Pete.

“Megamort!” says Minnie. “You’re just a mean ol’ meany villain.”

Megamort nods. “I’m taking the Clubhouse World for myself!”

He jumps in the zeppelin and takes off again.
