Megamort scrambles out of the flattened zeppelin.

“After all I did, I can’t believe you rescued me,” he says. “Thank you.”

“You’re welcome, Mr. Megamort,” says Goofy.

“I’m really Mortimer Mouse,” Megamort reveals. “I’m your new neighbor.”

“Well, you weren’t acting very neighborly,” Minnie points out.
Mortimer agrees. He reverses the shrink ray and returns Mickey
and Pete to their normal sizes!
