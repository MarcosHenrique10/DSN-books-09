When Mulan found Shang and the others,
no one would believe her story. Soon, the worst
happened: Shan- Yu captured the Emperor and
held him captive in the palace. Shan- Yu and
his men locked all the entrances. No one could
get inside to help the Emperor.

Finally, Mulan managed to convince
some of her army friends to help. Even Shang
eventually joined her. She formulated a plan
to disguise the men as women. The Huns
would never expect women to attack them.
Soon Mulan and her friends managed to
defeat Shan- Yu and save the Emperor— using
cleverness, not strength.
