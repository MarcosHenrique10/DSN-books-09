It began when the Huns attacked Fa Mulan’s
country, China. In times of war, men are called
to fight for their country. Sometimes, if a father
is called but is too weak to fight, his son may
volunteer to take his place. In Mulan’s family,
she is an only child. When her father was called
off to war, her heart was broken. He had fought
bravely in a previous war, and he had been
injured. He would not survive another battle.
Mulan did not know what to do.
