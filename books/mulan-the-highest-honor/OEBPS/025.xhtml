When she returned home, her family was
so happy to see her safe and sound. They were
deeply honored by the Emperor’s gifts.

And as for Shang, he found out where
Mulan lived, and he came to see her. She liked
that part. In fact, she asked him, “Would you
like to stay for dinner?”

And she felt very much in agreement when
Grandmother Fa added, “Would you like to
stay forever?”
