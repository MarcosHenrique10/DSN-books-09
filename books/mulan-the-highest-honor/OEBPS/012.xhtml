One day, Mulan and the men were running
through the mountains with weights across their
shoulders. Their captain, Shang, wanted them
to be strong so they would be prepared for battle.
But the weights were heavy, and Mulan could
barely keep up with the others. At last, she
tripped, falling to the ground. Captain Shang saw
her. He picked up her weights, carrying double
the load of anyone else. Mulan was humiliated as
she pulled herself to her feet. She was failing as a
solider, and she knew it.
