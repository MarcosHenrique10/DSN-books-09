Chapter Four

After the others had left, Mulan saw some
Huns emerge from the snow where they had
been buried by the avalanche. Among them
was Shan- Yu, their horrible and vicious leader.
Mulan knew she had to warn Shang and the
others, so she raced to the city where they were
meeting with the Emperor. They did not realize
Shan- Yu was hiding, ready to attack!
