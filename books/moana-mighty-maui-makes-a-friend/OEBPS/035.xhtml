Kalikolehua Hurley

was born and raised on Oahu, Hawaii. As a kid, she loved listening to stories from her Native
Hawaiian culture, especially those starring the mighty demigod Maui. When she isn’t writing,
she is teaching yoga, cuddling with her rescue pup, Kea, or packing for her next adventure.

Mehrdad Isvandi

is a visual development artist at Disney Animation Studios. He has worked on various projects,
including games, books, animated TV shows, and feature films. Mehrdad also loves oil painting
and playing music whenever he has free time. He lives in Los Angeles with his wife.
