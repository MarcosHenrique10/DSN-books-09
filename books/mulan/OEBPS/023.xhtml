The Emperor was taken to safety. Shan-Yu was furious. He charged
 toward Mulan.

Mulan ran in search of Mushu. “Quick,” she told him, “I have a plan.”

Mulan ran through the palace, making sure that Shan-Yu was
 following her. Then she led him onto the roof.
