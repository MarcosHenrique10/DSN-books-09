Suddenly, hundreds of flaming arrows flew down at them. Shan-Yu’s
 Hun army was charging toward Shang and his men!

“Fire the cannons!” yelled Shang. Soon only one cannon was left.

Mulan looked up at the snowy mountain peak and got an idea. She
 grabbed the last cannon and fired. The cannonball slammed into the
 mountain and shook the snow loose. An avalanche thundered down and
 swept the enemy away!
