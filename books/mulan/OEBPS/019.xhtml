Later, the army medic reported startling news: “This soldier
 isn’t a man—she’s a woman!” Serving in the army as a woman was
 a crime punishable by death.

“You deceived me,” Shang told Mulan angrily. “But I will spare
 your life because you saved mine.”

The troops marched off, leaving Mulan behind with Khan,
 Mushu, and Cri-Kee.
