When Mulan reached the army camp, she reported to
 Captain Shang. Shang was the son of General Li, leader of the
 Emperor’s army. It was Shang’s job to train the new recruits to be
 good soldiers.

“What’s your name?” Shang asked.

Mulan made up a boy’s name. “P-Ping . . .” she stammered.
 “My name is Ping.”
