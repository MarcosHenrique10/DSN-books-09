Mulan dressed her three soldier friends, Yao, Ling, and Chien-Po,
 in women’s clothes so that they could fool the Hun guards. Shang
 followed them, and they quickly overpowered the guards.

On a palace balcony, Shan-Yu waved his sword at the Emperor.
 “Bow to me!” he demanded.

At Mulan’s signal, the rescuers stormed into the room.
