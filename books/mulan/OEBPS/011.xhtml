A tiny dragon named Mushu raced after her. He wanted to
 regain his position as a Guardian of the Fa family. “I’ll make
 Mulan a hero! That will show the Ancestors!” he said.

A cricket named Cri-Kee hopped along beside him.
 “I can help,” he chirped. “I’m a lucky cricket.”

Mulan wasn’t sure what to think about Mushu and Cri-Kee.
 But she knew she needed all the help she could get.
