“My name’s Nemo. What’s yours?” Nemo asked the
 little fish.

“Eddy,” he cautiously replied. “You mean there’s no
 ghost fish?”

Nemo chuckled. “I thought you were the ghost fish,”
 he said. Then he explained the whole story. “By the way,”
 said Nemo, “how do you glow like that?”

Eddy shrugged. “I just do,” he said. “My whole family
 does.”

Nemo thought of someone who would know more
 about Eddy’s glow: Mr. Ray!
