Nemo swam closer to the shadow. There, cowering
 behind a rock, was a little fish, glowing softly. The ghost
 fish wasn’t a ghost fish at all! It was just a little glow-in-the-
 dark fish who had shined on a piece of coral and made the
 spooky shadow.

“Oh, hi!” Nemo called.

He wasn’t afraid anymore. But the little glowing fish was.
 It darted behind another rock. Then, timidly, it peeked out.

“Don’t be afraid,” Nemo said. “I’m just a little fish—like you.”
