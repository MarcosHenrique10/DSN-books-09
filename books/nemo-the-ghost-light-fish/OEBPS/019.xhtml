Nemo wanted to know what made Eddy glow.

Luckily, Mr. Ray knew the answer to that.

“See these patches on Eddy’s jaws?” said Mr. Ray. “Inside
 them are teeny-tiny glow-in-the-dark organisms. When
 you see Eddy glow, you’re really seeing those organisms
 glowing.”

“If you think that’s cool,” said Eddy, “you should see the
 rest of my family!”
