Mr. Ray took his students exploring all over the reef.
 Every day, Nemo and his classmates got an up-close look at
 different kinds of sea life.

That day, Mr. Ray was taking them to a clearing on the
 ocean floor.

“Okay, explorers,” Mr. Ray said when the class arrived,
 “now it’s time to do a little searching on your own. Let’s
 see if each of you can find a shell. Then we’ll identify them
 together!”
