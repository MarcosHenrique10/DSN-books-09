Only a few minutes passed before Nemo heard an
 odd noise. He looked up and saw Sheldon, Tad, and
 Pearl bolting out of the cave at full speed.

“What’s the matter?” Nemo asked. “Is it a
 barracuda? An eel?”

Sheldon shook his head. His eyes were big. His tail
 was quivering in fright. “No, w-w-worse!” he said. “It’s
 a g-g-ghost fish!”
