Nemo swam bravely into the cave.

See? he said to himself. Nothing to be afraid
 of here.

But just then, Nemo froze. On the cave wall
 next to him was a huge fish-shaped shadow!

Nemo took a deep breath. “Uh, excuse me,
 Mr. Ghost Fish? Or is it Ms. Ghost Fish?” he
 called.

“A ghost fish?!” a tiny voice said nervously.
 “Where? Don’t let it get me!”

Hmm . . . The ghost fish didn’t sound very
 scary, thought Nemo.
