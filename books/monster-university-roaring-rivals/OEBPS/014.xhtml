The first event of the Scare Games was a race through
a tunnel filled with stinging glow urchins. Whichever
team came in last would be out of the games.

Mike and Sulley tied in second place— but the rest of
the Oozma Kappas came in last. Then one of the other
teams got kicked out for cheating, which meant the
Oozma Kappas would go on to the next round!
