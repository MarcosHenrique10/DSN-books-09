“We’re going on a little field trip,” Mike said. The trip was to
Monsters, Inc., the factory where all the best Scarers worked.

Monsters, Inc. didn’t have just one kind of Scarer. They came
in all shapes and sizes. Every great Scarer was scary in his or her
own way.

At last, Sulley— and the Oozma Kappas— understood that a
monster didn’t need sharp claws and big teeth to be scary.
