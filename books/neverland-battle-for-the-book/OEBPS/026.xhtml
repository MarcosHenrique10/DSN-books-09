Back in London, Wendy reads everyone a story. “Jake had to stop
Hook from destroying the book. But Hook was very sneaky.”

“I love this story,” says Hook.

Wendy goes on. “But Jake and his mates were clever. They foiled the captain and got the book back! The end.”

Everyone cheers— except Hook, who loses
his balance and falls right out the window!

“Smee!” he wails. “Save me!”
