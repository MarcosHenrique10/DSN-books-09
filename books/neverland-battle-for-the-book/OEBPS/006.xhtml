Far away in Never Land, Hook and his men see Wendy
through a Magic Tide Pool. They listen to the story, too!

“Then,” Wendy continues, “the silly captain fell into the
Never Sea!”

Smee chuckles. “The captain never was a match for Peter Pan.”

“Her stories make me sound like a fool,” fumes Hook. “I’ll take
Wendy’s book and destroy it. Men, set a course for London!”
