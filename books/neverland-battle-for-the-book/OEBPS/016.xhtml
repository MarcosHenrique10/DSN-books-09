As Jake is about to grab Hook, the Jolly Roger splashes
down and throws everybody overboard, right on top of Jake
and his friends.

“What are you ruffians doing?” shouts a passing police officer.

“Me friends and I are being menaced by these ruffians,”
Hook says, pointing to Jake.

The policeman arrests Jake and his mates, and Hook
hurries back to his ship to head for Never Land.
