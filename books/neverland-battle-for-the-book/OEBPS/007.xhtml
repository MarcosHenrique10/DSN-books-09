The crew sews the ship’s sails together, fills them with hot
air, and the Jolly Roger floats all the way to London.

Just as Wendy finishes her story, Captain Hook jumps into
the room. “I’ll be taking this!” he says, grabbing the book.

“Stop, you wicked pirate,” yells Wendy as the boys
latch on to the book.

But Hook yanks it away and jumps out the window!
