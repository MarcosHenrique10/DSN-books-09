One night, Sulley was heading home at the end of his shift.
As he walked past the Scare Floor, he noticed a door was still
out. He thought it was odd, since the doors were usually put away
at night. He opened it and peeked inside to make sure a mon-
ster wasn’t still working. “Is anybody there?” he asked. No one
answered, so he closed the door.

“Boo!” something said. It was a little girl— and she was on
the Scare Floor!
