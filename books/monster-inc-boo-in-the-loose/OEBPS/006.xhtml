“Aaaah!” Sulley screamed. Every monster knew that nothing
from the human world was allowed into Monstropolis— it was too
dangerous for monsters. Recently, a monster had returned from
an assignment with a sock stuck to him. A whole squad had been
brought in to decontaminate him.

And now, a little girl was in
Monstropolis. Sulley knew he
had to get her out of there before
anyone saw her.

Sulley tried to put the girl
back into her room. Every time
he thought he had, she sneaked
back to the Scare Floor.

“Kitty!” she called to Sulley.

The monster had to do something— fast. If anyone found out
about her, he could lose his job!
