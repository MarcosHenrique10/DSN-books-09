The next morning, Sulley and Mike made a disguise for Boo:
a monster costume. “Be careful!” Mike cried. “Don’t let that kid
touch anything!”

They drove to the park, and Sulley and Mike got out of the
car. When Mike tried to open the door for Boo, he couldn’t.
She’d locked herself in!

“We have to get her out!” Mike said angrily.
