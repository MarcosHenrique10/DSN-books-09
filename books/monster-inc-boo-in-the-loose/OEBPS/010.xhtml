Sulley had an idea. He found a spare tire in the trunk and
squished Mike into the middle of it. Then he rolled it around.
“Fun!” he exclaimed, trying to get Boo’s attention.

“Yeah, fun,” Mike grumbled.

Boo just sat in the car.

Next, Sulley took out the car jack and cranked Mike up
and down.

“Whoa!” Mike yelled. “This
just keeps getting better.”

Boo still didn’t come out—
she was too busy playing with
Mike’s bear.

Then Sulley swung Mike
around in circles. “Don’t you
want to play, Boo?” he called.

Boo just waved at them.
