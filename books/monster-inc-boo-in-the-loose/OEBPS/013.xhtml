Sulley didn’t care— he missed Boo already. Then he thought
of something! If Mike thought they needed Boo, Sulley could
get her back. “We need to find Boo!” he exclaimed. “Her scream
will start the car.” He grabbed Mike’s teddy bear and ran into
the woods. “Boo?” he called. “I have your teddy bear. . . .”

Sulley looked and looked, but Boo was nowhere to be found.
Was she gone forever?

“Kitty?” a small voice finally said.

It was Boo!
She ran toward
Sulley and
hugged his leg.

The monster
smiled. She
did not seem
dangerous at all.
