There was only one monster Sulley could talk to— his best
friend and roommate, Mike. So, Sulley put the little girl in a bag
and sneaked her out of the building.

When Sulley got to the apartment, he took the girl out of
the bag. “Her name is Boo,” he explained.

“You named it?” cried Mike. He knew that the girl was
dangerous— and that he and Sulley would get in a lot of trouble
if anyone found out about her.
