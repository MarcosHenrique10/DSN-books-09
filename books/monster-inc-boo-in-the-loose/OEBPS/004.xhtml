Sulley was the top Scarer at Monsters, Inc. He worked on the
Scare Floor with his one- eyed friend, Mike, who was his scare
assistant. Sulley’s job was to scare human children. First Mike
would set up a closet door for Sulley to walk through. Once a
red light came on, Sulley opened the door and stepped into
a bedroom. Then he frightened the children inside until they
screamed loudly.

Next, Mike captured the screams and
sent them to the Monsters, Inc. factory.
There, the screams were converted into
energy, such as fuel for
cars or power for
lights. The whole
city of Monstropolis
used energy from
the factory.
