It was Gramma Tala!
 She believed the ocean’s
 gift was the heart of

Te Fiti!

As she grew, Moana worked hard to help lead
 her people and follow her father’s rules. But when
 Moana turned sixteen, Gramma Tala took her aside.
 “It’s time to learn who you were meant to be,”
 Gramma said. She led Moana to a hidden cavern . . .

. . . full of ancient canoes.
